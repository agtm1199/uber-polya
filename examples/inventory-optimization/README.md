# Inventory Optimization -- EOQ & Newsvendor

A retail store must decide how much to order, when to reorder, and how much to stock of a perishable product.

## Problem

A retail store sells a product with the following characteristics:

- **Annual demand**: 10,000 units
- **Order cost**: $50 per order
- **Holding cost**: $2/unit/year
- **Lead time**: 1 week (deterministic)
- **Weekly demand**: mean = 192 units, std = 40 units
- **Perishable variant**: unit cost = $10, selling price = $25, salvage value = $3

**Questions**:
1. What is the optimal order quantity (EOQ)?
2. What reorder point achieves a 95% service level?
3. What is the optimal newsvendor order quantity for the perishable variant?
4. How sensitive are these answers to parameter changes?

## Expected Output

- **EOQ**: ~707 units, total cost ~$1,414/year, ~14 orders/year
- **Reorder point**: ~258 units (with ~66 units safety stock)
- **Newsvendor Q**: ~222 units, critical ratio ~0.682
- **Monte Carlo fill rate**: within 3% of 95% target

## Key Concepts

- **Economic Order Quantity (EOQ)**: Balances ordering cost and holding cost to minimize total inventory cost
- **Reorder Point (ROP)**: Inventory level that triggers a new order, accounting for lead-time demand
- **Safety Stock**: Extra inventory held to buffer against demand variability during lead time
- **Newsvendor Model**: Single-period stocking decision for perishable goods under demand uncertainty
- **Critical Ratio**: Cu / (Cu + Co) -- the probability threshold for the newsvendor optimal quantity
- **Service Level**: Probability of not stocking out during a replenishment cycle

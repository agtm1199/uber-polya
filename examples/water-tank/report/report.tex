\subsection{Structure}

Design an open-top cylindrical tank to hold 1000 liters using the least material (minimize surface area). The tank consists of a circular base and a cylindrical wall with no lid. Given a required volume V, find the radius r and height h that minimize the total sheet-metal area S = pi*r\textasciicircum{}2 + 2*pi*r*h subject to the volume constraint pi*r\textasciicircum{}2*h = V.

% ── Solution Details ──────────────────────────────────────────────────
\subsection{Solution Details}


Method: Symbolic differentiation and critical point analysis via SymPy. The volume constraint V = pi*r\textasciicircum{}2*h is used to eliminate h from the surface area expression S(r, h), yielding a single-variable function S(r). Taking dS/dr and setting it to zero gives the optimal radius. The second derivative test confirms the critical point is a minimum. The closed-top variant (S = 2*pi*r\textasciicircum{}2 + 2*pi*r*h) is solved analogously for comparison.

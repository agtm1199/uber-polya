% ── Structure ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\subsection{Structure}

A software project has 5 phases, each with uncertain duration modeled as a triangular distribution (min, mode, max in days). Phases 1-3 are sequential; phases 4 and 5 run in parallel after phase 3 completes.

**Phase estimates (days):**

| Phase   | Min | Mode | Max | Dependencies     |
|---------|-----|------|-----|------------------|
| Phase 1 |  5  |   8  |  14 | None             |
| Phase 2 |  3  |   5  |  10 | Phase 1          |
| Phase 3 |  7  |  10  |  18 | Phase 2          |
| Phase 4 |  4  |   6  |  12 | Phase 3          |
| Phase 5 |  6  |   9  |  15 | Phase 3          |

**Critical path**: Phase 1 + Phase 2 + Phase 3 + max(Phase 4, Phase 5)

**Questions**:
- What is the expected total project duration (mean and 95\% CI)?
- What is the probability of finishing within 50 days?
- What is the Value at Risk at 95\% (worst-case duration at 95th percentile)?
- Which phase is the critical risk driver?

% ── Constraints ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\subsection{Constraints}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item $Monte Carlo simulation**: Estimate distributional properties of complex systems by repeated random sampling$
  \hfill \textit{()}
  \item $Triangular distribution**: Three-parameter distribution (min, mode, max) for expert elicitation of uncertain durations$
  \hfill \textit{()}
  \item $Critical path**: The longest dependency chain determines total project duration$
  \hfill \textit{()}
  \item $Value at Risk (VaR)**: The duration threshold exceeded only (1 - confidence)% of the time$
  \hfill \textit{()}
  \item $Conditional Value at Risk (CVaR)**: Expected duration in the worst-case tail beyond VaR$
  \hfill \textit{()}
  \item $Convergence analysis**: Demonstrates that Monte Carlo estimates stabilize as sample size grows$
  \hfill \textit{()}
  \item $Sensitivity analysis**: Correlation between individual phase durations and total duration reveals risk drivers$
  \hfill \textit{()}
  \item $Confidence intervals**: Quantify estimation uncertainty in the Monte Carlo mean via the Central Limit Theorem$
  \hfill \textit{()}
\end{enumerate}

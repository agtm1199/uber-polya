# Monte Carlo Project Risk Analysis

Estimate project completion time under uncertainty using Monte Carlo simulation with triangular duration distributions and dependency-aware scheduling.

## Problem

A software project has 5 phases, each with uncertain duration modeled as a triangular distribution (min, mode, max in days). Phases 1-3 are sequential; phases 4 and 5 run in parallel after phase 3 completes.

**Phase estimates (days):**

| Phase   | Min | Mode | Max | Dependencies     |
|---------|-----|------|-----|------------------|
| Phase 1 |  5  |   8  |  14 | None             |
| Phase 2 |  3  |   5  |  10 | Phase 1          |
| Phase 3 |  7  |  10  |  18 | Phase 2          |
| Phase 4 |  4  |   6  |  12 | Phase 3          |
| Phase 5 |  6  |   9  |  15 | Phase 3          |

**Critical path**: Phase 1 + Phase 2 + Phase 3 + max(Phase 4, Phase 5)

**Questions**:
- What is the expected total project duration (mean and 95% CI)?
- What is the probability of finishing within 50 days?
- What is the Value at Risk at 95% (worst-case duration at 95th percentile)?
- Which phase is the critical risk driver?

## Expected Output

- **Mean duration**: ~36 days
- **95% CI for mean**: narrow interval around the mean (via CLT)
- **P(finish <= 50 days)**: high probability (>90%)
- **VaR95**: 95th percentile duration (~43-44 days)
- **CVaR95**: conditional tail expectation above VaR95
- **Critical risk driver**: Phase 3 or Phase 5 (highest correlation with total duration)
- **Convergence**: running mean stabilizes well before 100,000 simulations

## Key Concepts

- **Monte Carlo simulation**: Estimate distributional properties of complex systems by repeated random sampling
- **Triangular distribution**: Three-parameter distribution (min, mode, max) for expert elicitation of uncertain durations
- **Critical path**: The longest dependency chain determines total project duration
- **Value at Risk (VaR)**: The duration threshold exceeded only (1 - confidence)% of the time
- **Conditional Value at Risk (CVaR)**: Expected duration in the worst-case tail beyond VaR
- **Convergence analysis**: Demonstrates that Monte Carlo estimates stabilize as sample size grows
- **Sensitivity analysis**: Correlation between individual phase durations and total duration reveals risk drivers
- **Confidence intervals**: Quantify estimation uncertainty in the Monte Carlo mean via the Central Limit Theorem
